SprigCast for Mac: test build
=============================

This is an early test build. SprigCast on a Mac starts, draws blooms from images, text, video files and
web pages, records, and streams with the CPU encoder.

Not on the Mac yet: microphone and desktop sound, screen and window capture, cameras by name, the virtual
camera, global hotkeys and in-app updates. They are being built.

Open it the first time
----------------------
This test build is not signed with an Apple developer account yet, so macOS asks before it opens:

1. Unpack the zip and move SprigCast to Applications (or leave it where it is).
2. Double-click SprigCast. macOS says it can't be opened. Click Done.
3. Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to SprigCast.
4. Open SprigCast again and click Open.

You only do this once.

Send us what you find
---------------------
In SprigCast: Help, Export report. It saves one zip with your logs (stream keys and personal
details are scrubbed). Share it in the SprigCast Discord: https://discord.gg/fgMD33XrhM

Logs live in ~/Library/Logs/SprigCast.
